What this version can do

Evergreen is a self-contained starter app. It works without an account and stores your entries in your browser. You can use the same app file on your phone and computer, but local entries remain separate until you manually export and import a backup. Automatic syncing, push notifications, Bevel integration, and bank connections are not included in this free starter version.
